# Right On Time Landing Page

Modern, clean, high-conversion landing page for the **Right On Time: Traffic Alarm App** (iOS).

**App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/right-on-time-traffic-alarm/id6779673858

**Privacy Policy:** https://rightontimeapp.github.io/privacy.html

**Developer:** Larry Reed (larritereed@gmail.com)

---

## Design Direction

- **Style:** Modern, minimal, clean. White background (#FFFFFF). High contrast black text. Subtle gray accents. Soft shadows. Plenty of white space.
- **Inspiration:** Apple product pages, Linear, Vercel, clean SaaS landings. Light, airy, professional.
- **Typography:** System fonts or Inter / SF Pro style. Bold headlines, readable body.
- **Colors:**
  - Primary: Deep blue or soft teal accent for CTAs and highlights (e.g. #007AFF or #0A84FF)
  - Background: Pure white
  - Text: Near black (#111111)
  - Secondary: Light gray (#F5F5F7)
- **Dynamic elements for conversion:**
  - Sticky header with "Download on the App Store" button
  - Hero with strong CTA
  - Scroll-triggered fade-in / slide-up animations on sections
  - Phone mockups that appear and gently float or scroll into view
  - Benefit cards that highlight on hover
  - Final CTA section with social proof
  - Smooth scroll, responsive, mobile-first

## Page Structure (Single Page)

1. **Header / Nav**
   - Logo: "Right On Time" (clean wordmark or simple icon + text)
   - Nav links: Features, How it Works, Pro, Download
   - Primary CTA button: Download on the App Store (Apple badge style)

2. **Hero Section**
   - Headline: "Arrive on time. Every time."
   - Subheadline: "Your adaptive traffic alarm clock. Right On Time uses live Apple Maps data to calculate exactly when you need to wake up and leave — then adjusts if traffic changes."
   - Primary CTA: Download on the App Store
   - Secondary: "See how it works" (scrolls down)
   - Right side or below: Large iPhone mockup showing the main app screen (traffic-aware alarm / trip card)

3. **Social Proof / Trust Bar**
   - 5.0 stars from App Store
   - "Takes away the guesswork" quote snippet
   - Privacy badge: "No accounts. No tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone."

4. **How It Works (3-step with mockups)**
   - Step 1: Tell us where, when, and how long you need to get ready
   - Step 2: We calculate your perfect wake-up and leave-by times using live traffic
   - Step 3: Alarms adjust automatically if conditions change
   - Each step has a phone mockup or illustration

5. **Features Grid / Benefits (with scroll mockups)**
   Highlight free + Pro:
   - Traffic-aware wake & leave alarms
   - Weekly schedule + one-time trips
   - Nightstand mode + ETA sharing
   - Calendar Auto-Commute (Pro)
   - Weather-aware buffers (Pro)
   - Unlimited profiles (Pro)
   - Automatic starting location updates (Pro)
   - Original alarm tones

   Use alternating left/right layout with phone mockups as you scroll.

6. **Pro Section**
   - Clean comparison or feature list
   - "Right On Time Pro"
   - Monthly / yearly / lifetime options mention
   - CTA to download and upgrade inside the app

7. **Testimonials**
   - Real App Store reviews (5.0 rating)
   - Quote cards

8. **Final CTA**
   - Big "Download Right On Time"
   - App Store badge
   - Short reminder of the promise: "Stop guessing. Start arriving on time."

9. **Footer**
   - Links: Privacy Policy, Terms (Apple EULA), Contact
   - Copyright Right On Time / Larry Reed
   - App Store link
